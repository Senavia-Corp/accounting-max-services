Investing and Taxes

Investing can have significant tax implications. Understanding how capital gains tax works is crucial for investors. Short-term capital gains are taxed at ordinary income rates, while long-term gains benefit from lower rates.

Strategies for Tax-Efficient Investing

Consider tax-advantaged accounts like IRAs and 401(k)s to minimize tax liabilities on your investments.
